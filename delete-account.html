🛡️ Google Play Data Safety Compliant

Delete Your Account & Associated Data

In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users of Ludo Playhouse have complete control over their personal data and can permanently delete their account and all game records at any time.

📱 Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Ludo Playhouse app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly with zero waiting time:

  • 1

    Open Settings or Your Profile

    Launch Ludo Playhouse and tap the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top right, or tap your avatar to open User Profile.

  • 2

    Navigate to "Account & Privacy"

    Scroll down to the ACCOUNT & PRIVACY card located at the bottom of the screen.

  • 3

    Tap "DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA"

    Tap the red DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA button.

  • 4

    Confirm Permanent Deletion

    Read the confirmation prompt and tap "YES, DELETE PERMANENTLY". Your cloud Firestore documents, friends list, inventory, wallet, and local SQLite data are wiped immediately.

⚡ Instant Execution: In-app deletion executes within seconds. Your session is terminated and your app resets to a fresh guest recruit state.
